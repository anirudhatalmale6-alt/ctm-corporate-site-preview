Three practices that work as one

Practice 01

Cloud

Cloud technology offers a wide range of benefits for businesses of all sizes, from cost savings to increased collaboration and scalability. With a variety of cloud services available, the hard part is not choosing a provider — it is choosing the right option for each workload, and being able to show why.

With the ever-growing demand for remote work and continued technology advancement, cloud is now part of how most organisations simply do business. We help you get there without inheriting someone else's architecture.

Talk about a cloud programme →
  • Readiness and business case — what moves, what stays, what it costs before and after.
  • Migration — sequenced by risk, with a tested route back at every step.
  • Cost optimisation — right-sizing, commitment planning and a bill you can explain to finance.
  • Landing zones and governance — identity, network and guardrails set up once, properly.
  • Operate — monitoring, patching and an on-call arrangement that matches your risk appetite.

Practice 02

Technology

Technology is constantly evolving and changing the way we live and work. From IoT to AI, VR/AR and cloud computing, new technologies have real potential to improve efficiency and convenience across industries.

Our job is to tell you which of them will do that for you — and to prove it on a workload that matters before anyone signs a three-year licence.

Scope a proof of concept →
  • AI and automation — use cases assessed on data readiness, not vendor demos.
  • IoT and connected assets — from device fleet to the data pipeline behind it.
  • VR / AR — training and field-service applications with a measurable outcome.
  • Integration — joining the systems you already own before buying another one.
  • Proof of concept to production — a defined path, with the exit criteria agreed up front.

Practice 03

Management

Having the right people in our company is essential to our customers' success. CTM enables the skills, experience and attitudes that help drive organisations forward, improve performance, increase productivity and foster a positive work environment.

That covers the people who run a programme and the ones who will still be running the system long after we have gone.

Discuss a resourcing need →
  • Programme and project management — delivery leadership that reports in plain language.
  • Service management — ITIL-aligned processes sized to your organisation, not to a textbook.
  • Capability uplift — coaching your team through the change rather than around it.
  • Vendor and contract management — holding suppliers to what they actually signed.
  • Interim leadership — experienced hands while you recruit permanently.
