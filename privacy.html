Privacy Policy

Elevate Technology Partners respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, company details and other information you provide when contacting us, requesting information or using our services.

We use this information to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide our services;
  • manage our customer and business relationships;
  • process transactions and provide support;
  • improve our website, services and customer experience; and
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, modification or disclosure.

We may use trusted service providers to assist with operating our business and providing our services. Where personal information is shared with third parties, we seek to ensure it is handled appropriately and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

You may contact us to request access to, or correction of, personal information we hold about you or to raise a privacy concern.
