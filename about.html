About us

A consultancy people keep calling back

Elevate Technology Partners works with organisations in Australia and the United Kingdom that need technology change to land — properly, and without drama.

Our purpose

Empower organisations to achieve their full potential

Elevate empowers businesses and organisations to achieve their full potential through innovative and cutting-edge technology solutions. We strive to be a leader in the industry by constantly pushing the boundaries of what is possible and delivering exceptional service to our clients.

In practice that means small, senior teams. The people who scope your work are the people who deliver it, and they stay reachable afterwards. There is no handover from a sales team to a delivery team, because there is no sales team.

It also means telling clients when the answer is "you don't need this". That has cost us work occasionally. It has also produced most of the relationships we still have.

How we operate

Our core principles

Integrity and a positive operational atmosphere are not a poster on the wall — they are the five things every person at Elevate is held to.

  • Fairness and openness — demonstrating fairness, honesty, integrity and openness, while respecting diverse opinions and treating everyone equally and with dignity.
  • Advocacy — advocating for Elevate's purpose, vision and values in all interactions, both internally and externally.
  • Compliance — adhering to the laws, regulations and professional standards relevant to each country in which Elevate operates.
  • Service — delivering a positive and valued experience to both internal and external recipients of our services.
  • Accountability — taking personal accountability for actions and decisions, aligned to Elevate's policies, procedures and authorised delegation of authority.

Trust

How we handle your information

We endeavour not to collect, hold, use or disclose personal information at all. Where collection is genuinely necessary and directly related to our functions, we always seek prior approval first.

  • Privacy Act 1988 — our privacy policy sets out how we comply with the Commonwealth Privacy Act.
  • ISO 27001 — Elevate controls all types of data in compliance with ISO 27001.
  • Purpose limitation — information is used only for the purpose it was given to us, or one directly related to it.
  • Access and correction — ask us what we hold about you and we will show you, or change it.
